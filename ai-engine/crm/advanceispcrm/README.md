# ISP CRM

## Operations and Digital theme

Use **Operations → Companies** to add company contact details and **Operations → Areas** to maintain service areas, with optional company, city and zone links. Active areas appear in customer create/edit forms and on the customer list/profile. Area renames update linked customer records; used areas/companies can be marked inactive instead of archived. Company grouping does not create tenant isolation. No company or area names are seeded automatically.

The **Operations** navigation item adds persisted payroll/attendance, internal work, vendor purchases and receiving, assets, network topology, bandwidth/reseller billing, cashbooks, customer requests, scheduled changes, collection approvals and in-app communications. Select **Digital** in the theme controls for the reference-inspired teal/navy workspace. It is the default for browsers without a saved preference.

The customer portal includes **Requests**, **Messages**, and **News & links**. CSV import and audited bulk status changes are available under Operations → Customer tools. The worker applies due CRM schedules and generates today's configured MAC-reseller daily tariff once.

**This is not complete feature parity with ISP Digital.** Branch directories do not impose branch isolation; separate reseller logins, native Android apps, complete accounting/regulatory formats, live router/OTT/payment/SMS services and financial discount adjustments remain gaps. PKR and the Pakistan provider choices are retained. See [ISP_DIGITAL_FEATURE_COVERAGE.md](ISP_DIGITAL_FEATURE_COVERAGE.md) for the precise delivered scope and limitations.

Normal startup initializes `operations_records` and `operations_events`. No demo records are inserted into the real database. Posted money/stock actions have idempotency and version checks; tests use isolated PostgreSQL schemas.

A local browser-based CRM built with Python, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and PostgreSQL.

With Python 3.10 or newer, install the database driver using `python -m pip install -r requirements.txt`. Ensure PostgreSQL is running, run `python ispcrm.py`, then open **http://localhost:3000** in your browser. Keep the terminal running; press Ctrl+C to stop. The server accepts connections only from this computer.

Connection settings are in `database.local.json` (excluded from Git). For another installation, copy `database.example.json` to `database.local.json` and set the password. Defaults are localhost:5432, user `postgres`, database `ispcrm`. Standard `PGHOST`, `PGPORT`, `PGUSER`, `PGPASSWORD`, and `PGDATABASE` environment variables override the file. Startup creates the database and tables if absent; the configured role needs permission to create the database on first run. Existing records are preserved on subsequent starts.

The original desktop prototype remains in `desktop_crm.py` and uses its separate SQLite database; use `ispcrm.py` for the PostgreSQL website.

- Customers: contact information, internet username, package, monthly fee, status.
- Inventory: quantities, minimum stock, cost, and location.
- Staff: contact information, role, area, and availability.
- Jobs: customer and staff assignments, type, status, and due date.
- Payments: customer receipts, billing month, method, and reference.
- Dashboard: active connections, expected fees, receipts received this month, jobs, staff, and low stock.
- Dashboard charts: daily/current-month or six-month receipt trends, customer status breakdown, active-customer revenue by package, upcoming/overdue jobs, and low-stock alerts. Cards open the matching records. Charts include readable data tables and work locally without external chart services. Monthly fees are expected revenue, not invoice balances or collected revenue. Run chart checks with `node tests/dashboard.test.cjs` if Node.js is available.
- Search, edit, delete, and export filtered records to CSV.
- Stock issues: use an inventory row's **Issue stock** button or **Stock issues → Issue stock**. Select an item, positive quantity, date, and employee responsible for the items. Issue against **Customer** (customer required), **System**, or **Network** (system/network destination required). Availability is reduced immediately in the same PostgreSQL transaction as the issue record; insufficient stock is rejected, including simultaneous requests. Issues are read-only history. **Return stock** restores the full issued quantity exactly once and marks the original record Returned; partial returns are not implemented. Correct an issue by returning it and creating another. Search or filter by destination type, employee, and status. Referenced inventory, customers, and employees cannot be deleted. Inventory quantities represent currently available stock; manual quantity edits can still be used to restock or reconcile counts.
- Expenses: record business costs with ISP categories (bandwidth, salaries, rent, electricity, fuel, equipment, fiber, maintenance, software, marketing), payee, amount, expense date, Paid/Pending/Cancelled status, payment method/date, reference, and notes. Edit or delete mistakes; mark cancelled bills to keep a record without including them in totals. Filter by expense month, status, category, or search, then export the matching list. A paid expense requires a payment date; pending/cancelled bills have no payment date. Amounts must be positive. Monthly recorded totals use expense dates; monthly paid totals use payment dates; pending totals include all dates. Dashboard net cash is recorded customer receipts minus paid expenses for the month, not accounting profit or bank balance. Package costs and inventory purchases are not posted automatically; enter actual expenses once to avoid double counting.
- Tickets: log customer issues, choose a type and priority, assign or reassign staff, set a due date, and track Open → In progress → Awaiting customer → Resolved → Closed. Staff choices show role, area, availability, and active ticket workload; suggested roles depend on issue type. New assignments to staff on leave are blocked. Assignment is required to start or resolve work, and resolution notes are required for Resolved/Closed. Tickets can be reopened; resolution timestamps are cleared on reopening. Assignment/status changes are recorded in ticket activity. Close tickets instead of deleting them. Filter by staff, status, and priority or search customer/staff names and ticket IDs. Dashboard highlights active, unassigned, and overdue tickets. Assignment saves locally; it does not send email or SMS, and staff can sign in through linked user accounts.
- Admin → Packages: create packages with speed, monthly price, installation fee, and active/inactive status. Select a package on customer forms to fill the monthly fee automatically. Price changes apply to new package assignments; existing agreed customer fees are preserved. Installation fees are catalog prices only, not automatically billed. Packages assigned to customers cannot be deleted or renamed; deactivate them instead. Existing customers with manually entered packages remain editable.
- Admin → Categories: add, rename, activate, deactivate, or delete inventory categories. Active categories appear in inventory forms. Renaming updates linked inventory automatically. Categories in use cannot be deleted; deactivate them to keep existing stock records while hiding the category from new assignments. The initial 52 ISP categories are loaded once, and existing inventory categories are preserved during the upgrade.

The CRM now requires sign-in. On the first visit, create the initial administrator account (there is no default password). In **Admin ? User management**, create accounts, enter a designation, link an employee, and choose a role. Passwords require 10?128 characters and are stored as salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes ([Python documentation](https://docs.python.org/3/library/hashlib.html#hashlib.pbkdf2_hmac)). Sessions use HttpOnly, SameSite cookies with an eight-hour expiry; log out when finished. Account changes or password resets revoke that user's sessions. The Password button lets each user change their password. The last active administrator cannot be deactivated or demoted.

Built-in roles:
- **Administrator:** all business modules plus user administration.
- **Manager:** all business modules and package/category settings; cannot manage accounts.
- **Accountant:** manage payments/expenses; read customer and package records.
- **Support:** manage customers/jobs/tickets; read staff and packages.
- **Technician:** update only assigned jobs/tickets; read customers, staff, inventory, and own stock issues. An employee link is required. Assignment, customer, priority, and due-date changes require a manager.
- **Storekeeper:** manage inventory and stock issues; read customer/staff/category lists.
- **Viewer:** read-only business access.

Designation suggests a role in the user form; administrators can override it. Permissions are enforced by the API, and unauthorized modules/data are excluded from responses. Role-specific home screens avoid showing incomplete business totals. Only administrators see password/account management. There is one account per linked employee; deactivate accounts instead of deleting them.
There is one **Admin** entry in the sidebar. Inside it, use **Packages & pricing**, **Inventory categories**, or **User management** to manage settings. Returning to Admin reopens the section you last used.

To log a ticket directly for a customer, open **Customers → Log ticket** on their row. The customer is preselected and their account details appear above the form. Enter the issue, select the employee in **Assigned to**, and save.

Dropdowns include a search box: type part of a name, ID, package, category, role, or status to narrow the options. Searches ignore case and accents and support multiple words. The selected value remains unchanged until you choose another option. Clear the search to see the full list; press Enter to select a single matching option, or Arrow Down to focus the dropdown. Search also works on dynamically refreshed employee choices and filter dropdowns.

Customer **Expiry date** defaults to **Joined + 30 calendar days**, including month/year boundaries. It is editable and cannot precede Joined. Changing Joined updates an automatic expiry; a manually adjusted expiry is preserved. Existing customers are backfilled only when the expiry field is missing. This stores a date; router suspension/automatic renewal is not implemented.

Each customer also receives a permanent, unique **9-digit Payment ID** beside their username. Existing customers and invoice snapshots are backfilled. PostgreSQL generates IDs from a noncycling sequence, enforces uniqueness and format, and blocks changes. IDs are searchable, exportable, and printed on invoices. This is an internal reference for a future payment integration; it is not yet an activated 1LINK biller/consumer number and does not initiate payments.

Customer documents can be selected when creating/editing a customer and are saved atomically with the customer record. After creation, use the row's **Documents** button to upload, download, or delete files. Document types include identity front/back, application form, installation photo, agreement, and other. Accepted formats: PDF, JPG/JPEG, PNG, and WebP, with matching file signatures. Limits: 5 files per upload, 5 MB per file, 20 MB total. Private file contents and metadata are stored in PostgreSQL, so database backups include them. Only Administrator, Manager, and Support roles may access documents; direct downloads require authentication. Customers with documents cannot be deleted until their documents are removed.

Use **Customers → Print invoice** (Administrator, Manager, Accountant, or Support) to open the monthly invoice in a new tab. Choose a billing month, then **Print / Save PDF**. One invoice is saved per customer/month. The first issue captures customer details, package, monthly charge, and the current customer expiry as its due date; reprints preserve that snapshot. The billing period is the selected calendar month and the charge is the full monthly fee, without proration or installation fees. Receipts for the matching customer and billing month determine payments received, remaining due, and any excess credit. Existing invoice snapshots cannot currently be amended or voided. Printing does not renew the connection, post a payment, or calculate previous-month arrears.

Packages also include **Monthly cost**: your monthly business cost per customer in PKR, separate from the monthly selling price and installation fee. Existing packages start at zero until you enter their actual cost. This field does not change customer charges.

For sample dashboard data, run `python seed_demo.py`. This adds clearly marked demo packages, 20 customers, 5 staff, 10 inventory items, 8 jobs, and 15 receipts for the current month. Existing records are preserved, and repeating the command skips existing demo records. Demo records affect dashboard totals until removed. Phone fields are deliberately non-dialable placeholders. To remove the samples manually, delete demo payments and jobs before their customers, staff, and packages.

Add customers and staff before entering jobs or payments. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD; billing months use YYYY-MM. Amounts are in PKR. Monthly expected fees are an estimate from current active customers, not an invoice balance. Payments are receipt records; invoices, arrears calculations, and automatic billing are not implemented.

Website data is saved in the PostgreSQL `ispcrm` database, under the `public` schema: `customers`, `inventory`, `staff`, `jobs`, and `payments`. Money is stored as exact decimal values. Use pgAdmin's Backup command on this database or `pg_dump` for backups. This version has local account logins and server-enforced roles. It has no router/RADIUS integration or messaging and remains bound to localhost. Inventory adjustments and staff availability are updated manually.

The database integration uses [Psycopg 3](https://www.psycopg.org/psycopg3/docs/basic/usage.html) with parameterized queries and transaction handling.


Customer accounts now have a compact list with advanced filters for payment ID/username, email, name, mobile, CNIC, type, and status. Select View profile to see the Internet subscription, private documents, saved invoices and payments, trouble tickets, work requests, issued hardware, and ticket activity according to your access role. Customer creation/editing includes optional email, CNIC, telephone, passport, and NTN fields, plus Residential/Business/Corporate type. Existing customers default to Residential. Balances cover saved invoices and payments matched by billing month. Additional IPTV/voice provisioning and payment gateway integrations are not implemented.


Customer recharge: Customers now offers Recharge / Recharge customer. Authorized finance users can confirm cash/card receipts and look customers up by their nine-digit payment ID. New current-month receipts automatically set the CRM customer status to Active once their total covers the agreed monthly fee. Expiry extends 30 days from the later of today and current expiry, once per billing month. Partial receipts accumulate. Past/future billing months do not automatically activate accounts. Existing historical payments are not automatically replayed during startup. Completed recharge receipts are immutable; online receipts are also immutable. Recharge history is visible under the customer Finance tab. These changes update CRM records only; router/RADIUS provisioning is not connected.

1LINK, Easypaisa and JazzCash native merchant connections remain pending the user's onboarding documents. A signed internal receipt bridge is implemented and disabled unless configured; it is not a provider-native callback. See [PAYMENTS.md](PAYMENTS.md) for the adapter contract and remaining integration work.


Every newly confirmed payment now saves a permanent receipt snapshot in PostgreSQL, in the same transaction as the payment and any recharge. Receipts have RCP numbers and preserve the customer nine-digit payment ID, internal payment record ID, customer details, amount, method, reference, payment date, billing month, recorder, and account/expiry state. Open Receipt / Print from Payments, or the receipt number under Customer > Finance > Payments; the print view supports browser Save as PDF. Existing payments receive clearly labelled historical receipts on migration. Saved payment receipts and their source payments cannot be edited/deleted through the CRM. Customer edits do not change saved receipts. Duplicate online notifications or a retried manual request reuse the same receipt. Receipt data follows Payments read permissions.

Partial activation: Administrator, Manager and Accountant can choose "Activate now and carry unpaid amount to next bill" while recording a partial payment. The default keeps account status unchanged until the full due amount is paid. Full payment of the current monthly subscription plus prior unpaid invoiced balance automatically activates the customer. Approved partial activation renews expiry once, records the approving user and unpaid amount in recharge history, and preserves the decision on the payment receipt. The current invoice is saved when processing a current-month recharge so the debt is retained even when the operator never opened Print invoice. Later payments settle the balance without another renewal in the same month.

Invoices now show monthly charge, previous unpaid balance and total bill separately. Previous unpaid balance is calculated from earlier monthly charges minus receipts matched to those invoiced months, so settlements after printing also reduce future bill arrears. Carried amounts are not added again to monthly subscription charges or global invoice totals. For example: Rs 2,000 due, Rs 800 paid and partial activation approved leaves Rs 1,200 unpaid; the next Rs 2,000 bill shows Rs 3,200 total. Paying Rs 3,200 settles the debt and the following monthly bill returns to Rs 2,000. Uninvoiced months are not automatically charged as arrears.


Payments now have a Revenue type: MRC (monthly subscription bills) or OTC (one-time router, inventory, installation or other charges). Existing payments default to MRC. Dashboard cards and payment summaries show MRC and OTC receipts separately by payment date. OTC requires a description in Reference, has its own saved receipt, and does not reduce monthly invoices/arrears or activate subscriptions. Stock movements are still recorded separately through Stock issues. Provider bridge receipts currently default to MRC.


Jobs have been consolidated into Tickets. Use ticket types Installation, Complaint, Maintenance and Collection for employee work alongside existing support/billing types. Tickets support assignment, due dates, notes, priority, resolution and activity history, plus a type filter. Existing jobs migrate once into tickets with their original assignments, notes and due dates; Completed jobs become Resolved tickets. Original Jobs rows remain archived in PostgreSQL and are not editable through the CRM. The dashboard and customer account now use the unified ticket workflow.


Tickets now include turnaround targets (TAT), precise PKT deadlines, elapsed time, remaining/overdue time, and within-target/breached resolution indicators. New ticket priority defaults are Urgent 2h, High 4h, Normal 24h and Low 48h. Authorized staff can adjust targets from 1 to 720 calendar hours; technicians cannot change them. The clock runs continuously from creation (no business-hour or awaiting-customer pauses), and reopening preserves the original clock. Existing tickets retain their date-based deadlines at 23:59:59 PKT. Manage a ticket to see its chronological timeline, including staff names on new activity and audited target changes. Time indicators refresh every minute.


Issue stock directly from Customer > Issue stock (also available in Hardware details and the customer row More menu). The form pins the customer, requires an employee and shows available inventory. Saving atomically records the issue and deducts stock, then returns to the customer Hardware details. Out-of-stock options are disabled; insufficient quantities and duplicate retry deductions are prevented by the server. Cancelling returns without an issue or deduction.


Customer > Activation costs now shows net issued inventory, returned inventory value, additional expenses (Paid + Pending, excluding Cancelled), and the combined recorded setup cost. Add installation labour, transport and other additional costs through Add activation expense, which allocates the expense to that customer. Avoid entering stock acquisition costs again: issued materials are already included at their unit cost captured on issuance. New issue costs stay unchanged if inventory prices change. Older issues are backfilled using current inventory cost and clearly marked Historical estimate; zero-cost outstanding issues are flagged as unpriced. Recurring package costs are displayed separately, and package installation selling fees are not treated as business costs. Only roles with Expenses read permission can view this report. Totals cover recorded stock and allocated expenses, including all customer issue history, rather than inferred/unrecorded costs.


The dashboard includes Sales, billing & collections with independently selected daily date and monthly period. Each shows total sales, MRC bills raised, OTC paid sales, MRC bill collections, OTC collections, total collections, paid expenses and net cash, plus bill counts, joined customers and recharge/activation counts. Expand monthly payment-method totals and the daily breakdown to reconcile figures. Sales use invoice issue dates plus paid OTC charges; collections use payment dates. Carried arrears are excluded from new sales totals. Outstanding billed balances are netted per customer through the selected daily date, including settlements in later billing months. Unbilled months and unpaid OTC orders are not inferred. Net cash is receipts minus paid expenses, not profit. Finance-readable roles also receive the financial dashboard on their role home page; other roles receive no invoice/recharge dashboard data.


The company dashboard overview now uses a responsive four-column coloured card layout. It includes total/active/new clients, current billing-month MRC receivable and pending bills, monthly and daily collections, total billed balance, expiry alerts (today/3/7 days), MRC/OTC collection splits, open tickets and low stock. Expiry cards count active customers inclusively from today and show their monthly package costs and monthly fees, not inventory purchase prices. Customize Dashboard controls card visibility and stores the layout per login in the current browser. Existing detailed sales, collections and operational charts remain below the overview.


Customer termination and recovery: Customer > Terminate account records a reason, termination time, monthly fee run-rate lost and optional recovery employee. The customer becomes Terminated; required equipment remains Equipment recovery pending until every recoverable customer stock issue has been returned. Use Equipment recovery > View recovery > Recover and return to stock to record physically returned full issue quantities. Stock restores once, pending status clears when all required items are returned, and the linked Equipment recovery ticket resolves automatically. Ordinary ticket resolution cannot bypass outstanding equipment. The account remains Terminated after recovery until an authorized user explicitly reactivates it. Payment receipts for terminated accounts do not reactivate them; new stock issues and new subscription bills are blocked.

Stock issue forms include Recovery required (Yes/No). New UI selections suggest Yes for devices and No for consumables; the operator can override for company-owned versus customer-owned/sold equipment. Older issues are classified from their inventory category on migration. Returning a complete issue requires all units in that issue to be physically recovered. No equipment is restored merely by terminating an account. Existing Disconnected customers are not retrospectively treated as new terminations.

Dashboard overview and daily/monthly financial periods include termination counts, equipment-recovery pending counts, and lost monthly MRC run-rate captured at termination. This is a gross monthly recurring-revenue reduction for termination events in the selected period, not daily cash lost or prorated revenue. Duplicate termination submissions reuse the same event; reactivation followed by a later termination creates another auditable event. Sales and cash receipt figures are not rewritten by these termination metrics.


Customer deployment pins: Latitude and Longitude are required when creating a customer through the CRM, and editable afterward in Edit customer. Existing records with no pin are retained without fabricated coordinates; older API updates preserve omitted pins. Coordinates are stored to seven decimal places with pair and geographic-range validation. The editor accepts pasted latitude/longitude and can request current browser location only after the operator clicks Use current location (use it at the installation site). Customer details shows the saved coordinates and a View deployment on map link. The external map opens only when clicked. This location belongs to the customer installation, not to staff login accounts.

Installation charges: new customers default to the package installation fee; the agreed customer charge can be adjusted (zero waives it). Existing customers start at zero to avoid creating retrospective charges. Customer Finance shows installation charged, paid and outstanding. Use Record installation payment, or Payments > OTC > Installation, to save a confirmed payment and permanent receipt. Installation receipts count as OTC collections and do not settle MRC bills or renew connections. Charges cannot be reduced below the installation amount already received; installation overpayments are rejected.

Reports: finance-readable roles can open Reports and select Monthwise Collection, Collection Reports (daily totals), Promise Date Report, Expiry Report, Staff Collection (receipt recorder), Collection Report 1 (method totals), Accounts Report, Daily Sales Report, Transaction Wise Report, Recharge Report, or Package Wise Report. Each has date filters, text search, CSV and print. Promise dates are optional customer follow-up dates. Accounts uses the end date and saved invoice balances; package groups use current packages. OTC sales use paid receipts. Report descriptions state date and calculation rules.

Automatic expiry recovery: while the CRM server runs, a background check every minute opens an unassigned Equipment recovery ticket 5 calendar days after customer expiry (Pakistan time), with a 24-hour TAT. State refresh also checks overdue accounts. One ticket is retained per customer expiry cycle, preventing duplicates. Successful current-month recharge (including authorized partial activation) resolves the ticket; ordinary incomplete payments or OTC receipts do not. Returning all recovery-required issued stock also resolves it. Returning only some devices keeps it open. Terminated accounts retain their separate termination recovery workflow. Existing overdue accounts are included on the next check.

Admin > Ticket TAT: administrators and managers can edit default hours (1?720) for each ticket type and priority. Defaults are used for new manual and automatic recovery tickets. Existing ticket deadlines remain unchanged until edited individually in Tickets > Manage; technicians cannot change existing targets. TAT measures continuous calendar hours from original creation.

Staff registration includes employee code, parent name, CNIC, birth date, gender, email/alternate phone, address, emergency contacts, designation, department, employment type, joining date, agreed monthly salary, allowances, deductions, bank details and notes. Net salary is calculated from the saved amounts. Admin/Manager can view private registration and pay details; other staff-readable roles receive only operational fields. Existing staff records remain editable and old API updates preserve the new data. Salary figures are agreed monthly pay, not payroll disbursements; record actual salary payments in Expenses.

Inventory QR tracking: Inventory > Register / Receive QR device creates a unique printable QR for each physical device (optional serial/MAC). Label existing stock without changing quantity, or receive new to add one unit. QR device register lists labels and movement histories. Scan with a USB/Bluetooth scanner that types the code and Enter, paste a code, or use a camera on browsers supporting BarcodeDetector (camera permission required). Scanning identifies the device; confirm Issue with customer/system/network, employee and details, or confirm physical Return. Stock moves atomically with immutable received/issued/returned history and recorded operator; duplicate requests do not duplicate stock. QR-registered devices cannot be issued via the bulk untracked stock quantity. The Stock issues form, including customer stock issuance, also has a QR input. Bulk fibre and other consumables can continue using normal quantity issues. Camera decoding is browser-dependent; keyboard scanners work without camera support. QR generation stays local; no inventory data is sent to an external QR service.

Inventory controls: use Inspect / Change condition on a device to mark Good, Damaged or Under repair with notes. Damaged/repair units cannot be issued; their physical store quantity is retained. Inventory > QR stocktake opens/resumes one shared count: scan present QR devices, review expected/unscanned/unexpected and movement changes, then close to freeze results. Stocktakes never adjust inventory automatically, and unlabelled bulk stock is outside the QR count. Reopen saved counts by number. Reports > Inventory Reconciliation shows store stock, QR issued/in-store, repair quantities, unlabelled balance and stock valuation; inventory-readable roles can access this report.

Customer app: open /customer/ for a mobile customer portal and installable web app (browser support and HTTPS/localhost required). In Customers > View profile > Customer app access, staff with customer write permission can generate/reset a temporary password; the customer signs in with their 9-digit payment ID and must change it. Sessions/cookies are separate from staff access. The portal shows only that customer's balance, expiry, invoices with printing, receipts, ticket statuses and in-app notifications. Staff can send a notice with Send app notification. Bills, confirmed receipts, expiry and ticket status alerts are generated when the portal refreshes; the visible app refreshes each minute. Out-of-app web push, WhatsApp/SMS, native app-store packages and online payment checkout are not connected by this feature. Host on a stable public HTTPS address for customers outside the office.
