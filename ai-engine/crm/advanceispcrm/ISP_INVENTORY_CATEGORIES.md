# ISP / FTTH inventory categories

50 active categories added to the local CRM.

Manage them under **Settings ? Inventory categories**. Choose them when creating records under **Inventory ? + Add inventory**.

Groups below organize this reference; the CRM category dropdown is a flat list.

## Miscellaneous

- Other

A category describes a type of stock. Record the manufacturer, model, quantity, unit cost, selling price and store location on the inventory item itself.

When issuing stock, check Recovery required: Yes for company equipment that must be returned; No for consumables or customer-owned equipment.
