Remove SNMP device manager and syslog; monitoring is ping + traceroute only

Monitoring keeps exactly what it had that worked: ICMP ping with the latency
graph, traceroute and the port check. Everything that depended on an SNMP walk
of a third-party device, or on a syslog listener, is gone.

Backend
 - deleted src/ndm/ (14 files): the whole network-device manager -- devices,
   interfaces, ports, alerts, alert rules, syslog events, forward targets and
   the on-disk syslog archive.
 - deleted telemetry/snmp-poller.service.ts and telemetry/syslog-receiver.service.ts.
 - telemetry.controller.ts: removed GET nas/:id/discover-interfaces,
   GET nas/:id/signals and GET onu/:id/signal.
 - nas-monitor.service.ts: removed ONU optical signal sampling. Traffic
   sampling is untouched -- it reads radacct, never SNMP, so the NAS traffic
   graphs keep working exactly as before.
 - monitoring.service.ts: unifiedList() no longer queries networkDevice, alert
   or syslogEvent. The response SHAPE is preserved (snmp/ports/syslog null,
   counters zero) so nothing downstream has to change at once.
 - route-permissions.ts: dropped the now-dead discover-interfaces entry.

Kept deliberately
 - telemetry/device-health.service.ts. It does its own SNMP walk for NAS CPU,
   memory, temperature and response time and never depended on the poller, so
   the NAS Health tab and the "Test SNMP" button still work.
 - the database tables (NetworkDevice, SyslogEvent, OnuSignalSample and the
   rest). Dropping them would destroy real historical readings to tidy up a
   removed feature; they simply stop being written. The nightly prune is left
   in place so they drain on their own retention schedule.

Frontend
 - removed /monitoring/{devices,ports,syslog,events,alerts,settings} and the
   ndm.ts / ndm-ui.tsx / ndm-tabs.tsx / ndm-sound.tsx modules behind them.
 - monitoring/page.tsx: dropped the network-device panel, the SSE ndm:alert
   port-alert cards and the SNMP/ports/syslog capability chips. Details now
   always opens the ping history page.
 - NAS pages: removed the Syslog tab, the syslog config fields, the ONU
   optical-signal list and the SNMP interface-discovery button.

Verified
 - backend: tsc clean in the touched files, 50 suites / 748 tests pass.
 - frontend: tsc --noEmit exits 0, 10/10 unit tests pass.
